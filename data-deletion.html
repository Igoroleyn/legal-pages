Data Deletion Instructions

Market Radar Threads Research · Last updated: 10 October 2026

If you have used, authorized, or been affected by the application Market Radar Threads Research and wish to request deletion of personal information associated with you, please follow these steps.

  1. Send an email to igcontentfactory@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request – Market Radar Threads Research.
  2. Describe the relevant Threads or Instagram account, post, or other information you would like us to locate and delete. Do not include your password, API token, authentication code, or identity documents unless we specifically explain why verification is necessary through a secure method.
  3. We will review the request, may ask for reasonable verification of account ownership or identity, and explain what information can be deleted and whether a legal or platform retention requirement applies.

You may independently review or revoke the application's permissions in your Meta account settings. Revoking authorization can prevent future account access but may not itself remove data previously retained by a separate application.

For further details, see the Privacy Policy.
