The application is designed for authorized research of publicly available discussions on Threads and for optional integrations with Instagram. 3. Information that may be processed

When a feature is enabled, relevant data may include publicly accessible post content, usernames, profile identifiers, post identifiers, timestamps, public engagement metrics, search keywords and results, and limited technical information used to operate, secure, or troubleshoot API connections. For connected accounts, permission-dependent profile or account information may also be processed. This does not imply that all of these categories are collected in every use of the application.

We do not request account passwords through this website. Any access authorization takes place through the relevant platform's authorization process.

The intended purposes are to research topics and public discussion trends, display relevant findings to an authorized operator, maintain connections expressly enabled by an account holder, resolve technical errors, and comply with applicable law and platform terms.
